# Data Retention and Privacy Policy

## Retention Periods

Application logs are retained for 30 days. Audit logs are retained for 1 year by default and for 7 years on the Enterprise plan.

Database backups are taken daily and kept for 35 days. Backups are encrypted with AES-256 and stored in a different region from the primary database.

## Deleting Data

When a workspace is deleted, its data is removed from production systems immediately and purged from backups within 35 days as the backups expire.

## Privacy Requests

We fulfil data subject requests under the GDPR, such as access or erasure, within 30 days. Requests can be sent to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@northwind.example.

## Subprocessors

We publish the list of subprocessors on our trust page and notify customers 30 days before adding a new subprocessor.
