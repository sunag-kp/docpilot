# Northwind Labs Employee Handbook

## Working Hours

Our standard working week is 38 hours. Most teams work between 08:30 and 17:30, but you may arrange a different schedule with your manager as long as you are reachable during core collaboration hours.

Overtime is not expected as a habit. If you work more than 45 hours in a week because of an incident or a launch, record it in the HR portal and take the equivalent time back within the next two months.

## Paid Time Off

Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of paid time off for every month of service, which adds up to 21 days per year. Part-time employees accrue PTO in proportion to their contracted hours.

You may carry over up to 8 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire on March 31 if they have not been used by then.

Requests for more than five consecutive days must be submitted at least three weeks in advance. Shorter requests need one week of notice. Your manager approves all requests in the HR portal.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 12 paid sick days per calendar year. Unused sick days do not carry over and are not paid out.

If you are sick for more than three consecutive working days, you must provide a doctor's note to HR. Please tell your manager as early as possible on the first day you are unwell.

## Parental Leave

The primary caregiver receives 18 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time within the first year after the birth or adoption.

Parental leave can be combined with PTO. Employees returning from parental leave may work a reduced 80 percent schedule at full pay for their first month back.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone; please keep meetings within this window.

New remote workers receive a one-time home office stipend of 400 euros for a desk, chair or monitor. Internet costs are reimbursed up to 30 euros per month.

Working from another country for more than 20 working days per year requires approval from the People team because of tax and insurance rules.

## Expenses

Submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase through Ledgerly, our expense tool, and attach a receipt for every item.

Meals while travelling are covered up to 45 euros per day. Book economy class for flights shorter than six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights.

## Learning and Development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,200 euros for courses, books and conference tickets. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Code of Conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Report harassment, discrimination or other concerns to ethics@northwind.example; reports can be anonymous and retaliation is never tolerated.
