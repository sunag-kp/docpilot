# Service Level Agreement

## Uptime Commitment

Northwind commits to a monthly uptime of 99.95 percent for Enterprise customers and 99.9 percent for Growth customers. The Starter plan has no uptime commitment.

Uptime is measured by external probes every minute from three regions. A minute counts as down when more than 5 percent of probe requests fail.

## Service Credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, customers receive a credit on the next invoice. Uptime below 99.95 percent but at least 99.0 percent earns a credit of 10 percent of the monthly fee. Uptime below 99.0 percent but at least 95.0 percent earns a 25 percent credit. Uptime below 95.0 percent earns a 50 percent credit.

Credits must be claimed through the support portal within 30 days after the end of the affected month. Service credits are the sole remedy for missing the uptime commitment.

## Support Response Times

Enterprise customers can raise P1 incidents 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, and receive a first response within 15 minutes. P2 incidents receive a response within 4 business hours, and P3 requests within 2 business days.

Growth customers receive email support during business hours with a first response within one business day.

## Maintenance Windows

Planned maintenance takes place on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC. Customers are notified at least 72 hours in advance, and planned maintenance does not count as downtime.

## Exclusions

The commitment does not cover outages caused by the customer's own systems, force majeure events, or features labelled as beta.
