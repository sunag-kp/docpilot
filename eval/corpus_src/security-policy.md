# Information Security Policy

## Passwords and Authentication

All passwords must be at least 14 characters long. We do not force periodic password changes; you must change a password immediately if you suspect it has been exposed.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every company account. Administrators must use a hardware security key as their second factor; authenticator apps are not sufficient for admin access.

Never reuse your company password on any other website, and store all credentials in the approved password manager.

## Device Security

Full-disk encryption (FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows) is mandatory on every laptop that accesses company data. Screens must lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity.

If a laptop or phone with company data is lost or stolen, report it to security@northwind.example within one hour so the device can be wiped remotely.

## Access Reviews

Managers review the system access of their team members every quarter. Access that is no longer needed is removed within five working days of the review.

When an employee leaves, all of their accounts are disabled on their last working day.

## Data Classification

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment details and health information, may only be accessed with written approval from the data owner.

Confidential and Restricted data must never be pasted into unapproved third-party tools.

## Vulnerability Management

Critical vulnerabilities must be patched within 7 days of disclosure. High-severity vulnerabilities must be patched within 30 days, and medium-severity ones within 90 days.

Exceptions require a documented risk acceptance signed by the CISO.

## Security Training

All employees complete security awareness training when they join and once a year afterwards. We also run a simulated phishing exercise every month; anyone who clicks a simulated phishing link is enrolled in a short refresher course.
